Mass Timber Hackathon

Following the symposium, dive into a weekend of intensive collaboration and innovation. Organized by the AEC Hackathon team, this event focuses on advancing mass timber construction through technology and sustainable practices.

What is an AEC Hackathon?

Building on Global Timber Innovation

Following successful timber-focused hackathons in Zurich and the Digital Prefabrication Challenge, it's now time for Oregon and the Pacific Northwest to lead in timber technologies.

With our region's rich forestry heritage, innovative manufacturing capabilities, and thriving tech ecosystem, we're uniquely positioned to pioneer the next generation of sustainable mass timber solutions. Join us in advancing digital fabrication, AI-driven design, and sustainable construction practices that will shape the future of building with wood.

Challenge Areas

Digital Prefabrication

Optimize manufacturing workflows and quality control systems

DfMA

Create tools for optimizing mass timber design for efficient manufacturing and assembly processes

Construction Tech

Develop solutions for on-site assembly and project management

AI in Mass Timber

Leverage artificial intelligence for design optimization, quality control, and predictive analytics
